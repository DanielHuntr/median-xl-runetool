# Median XL Runetool

A companion site for **Median XL 2.14**: look up items, plan a character, and keep your builds and loot filters in one place. Its numbers come from the game's own files wherever they can, so what you see matches what you'll see in game.

## What you can do

### Look things up

- **Runeword Finder**: find runewords by name, base or stat. Tell it which runes you own and it shows what you can make now, and what you're one rune away from.
- **Tiered Uniques**: every tiered unique, with what each tier upgrade brings.
- **Sacred Uniques** and **Sets**: endgame uniques on sacred bases, and sacred sets with their set bonuses.
- **Gems & Runes**: what every gem and rune adds in a weapon, armour or shield.
- **Base Items**: every base item, from Tier 1 to Sacred.
- **Cube Recipes**: put items in the Horadric Cube and see what the game makes of them, straight from its recipe table.

Favourite anything to find it again, and compare tiers side by side.

### Plan a character

The **Character Planner** puts your whole character on one screen, laid out like the game:

- **Attributes and skills**: spend stat points and skill points, with tooltips that read like the in-game ones, at the right level.
- **Equipment**: fill each slot with uniques, set items, runewords, base items or your own item, with roll sliders, sockets, charms and relics.
- **Stats**: life, mana, resistances, damage, attack and cast speed breakpoints and more. Select any number to see where it comes from.
- **Mercenary**: hire one, gear it up and add its auras to your stats.
- **Suggest gear**: picks items that suit your skills, and explains why.
- **Levelling stages**: keep a version of the build for Normal, Nightmare, Hell and Endgame, or add your own stages (say, "Normal (alternatives)") and drag them into order. Each stage shows where to level and which new runewords open up.

Everything is connected: +skills on gear raise your skills, passives add their stats, set bonuses switch on as you equip pieces, and anything you can't wear yet is flagged.

### Save and share builds

- **Save build** keeps a build in your browser; **Share build** copies a link anyone can open.
- The **Builds** page lists your builds, and **community builds** other players have published. Like the ones you rate and sort by most liked.

### Loot filters

Browse the community filters from the median-xl.com Filter Exchange, then edit a copy or make your own. Filters export in the same JSON the game's filter uses.

### Your account (optional)

Sign in with Google to:

- keep your builds and loot filters on any device;
- publish builds to the community under a display name you choose (your Google name and email are never shown);
- like other players' builds.

Everything works without an account; your builds and filters are then kept in your browser. Your account page lets you rename or delete anything, or delete the account itself. See the site's Privacy page for what's stored and where.

## Themes

Pick from standard, light and high-contrast themes, or Diablo II-inspired ones (Hell, Arreat Summit, Horadric, Kurast and more), from the theme menu.

## Found a mistake?

Use **Report a bug** or **Suggest an idea** under *More* in the site's menu. A value that doesn't match the game is the most useful thing to report: a screenshot of the in-game tooltip helps a lot.
